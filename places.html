21+ Destinations Across Odisha

Mountains and wildlife, coastline and lagoons, temples and ancient monasteries — grouped so you can plan by mood.

Mountains, Waterfalls & Wildlife

Deomali Hills (Koraput)

Odisha's highest peak, offering panoramic tribal and natural views.

Daringbadi (Kandhamal)

Known as the "Kashmir of Odisha" for its pine forests and cool climate.

Mahendragiri (Gajapati)

Second-highest peak, steeped in mythology, ideal for trekking.

Barehipani Falls (Similipal)

Spectacular two-tiered waterfall in the core of Similipal National Park.

Khandadhar Falls (Sundargarh)

Odisha's second-highest waterfall, known for its natural pool.

Koraput & Tribal Museum

Centre of tribal culture and lush green valleys in southern Odisha.

Satkosia Gorge & Tiger Reserve

The Mahanadi cutting through the Eastern Ghats — gharials and eco-tourism.

Similipal National Park (Mayurbhanj)

Major tiger and biosphere reserve, host to beautiful waterfalls.

Coastal Beaches & Lagoons

Puri Beach (Golden Beach)

Blue Flag certified beach, renowned for sand art and serenity.

Chilika Lake (Largest Lagoon)

Asia's largest brackish lagoon — migratory birds and Irrawaddy dolphins.

Chandipur Beach (Balasore)

The "vanishing sea" — water recedes up to 5 km at low tide.

Talasari Beach (Balasore)

Scenic beach known for red crabs and palms, near the Subarnarekha estuary.

Gopalpur-on-Sea (Ganjam)

Historic port town with a serene beach and a major lighthouse.

Bhitarkanika National Park

Largest population of saltwater crocodiles and mangrove forests.

Chandrabhaga Beach (Konark)

Stunning sunrises, close to the Sun Temple.

Ancient, Religious & Historical Sites

Konark Sun Temple

UNESCO World Heritage Site — 13th-century chariot temple architecture.

Lingaraj Temple (Bhubaneswar)

One of the oldest and largest temples dedicated to Lord Shiva.

Dhauli Shanti Stupa

Buddhist peace pagoda over the ancient Kalinga War battleground.

Panchalingeswar Temple (Balasore)

Hilltop Shiva temple where five Shivalingas are bathed by a natural stream.

Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves

Ancient Jain rock-cut shelters dating to the 2nd century BCE.

Ratnagiri, Lalitgiri & Udayagiri

The "Diamond Triangle" — ancient Buddhist monasteries and stupas.
