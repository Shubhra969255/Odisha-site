Before you book

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer self-drive rentals, or is a driver mandatory?

Both. Sedans, SUVs and bikes can be rented self-drive with a valid licence and deposit, or with a driver at no extra charge for trips outside city limits.

How far in advance should I book a tour package?

3–5 days is usually enough, but during Rath Yatra (Puri) and major festival weekends, we recommend booking 2–3 weeks ahead as vehicles and stays fill up fast.

Are permits needed for Similipal or Bhitarkanika?

Yes — both are protected forest areas requiring an entry permit, which we arrange for you as part of any package that includes them.

What's the best time of year to visit Odisha?

October to March is the most comfortable — cool, dry weather for the coast and hill stations alike. Daringbadi can get quite cold in December–January.
