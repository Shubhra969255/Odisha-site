Our Best Tour Packages

Fixed itineraries covering the coast, the temple towns and the tribal hill country — ask us to customise any of these.

Golden Triangle Tour 3D/2N · from ₹9,500 pp

Bhubaneswar – Konark – Puri. Temples, chariot architecture and the coast in one easy loop.

  1. Day 1: Arrive Bhubaneswar, visit Lingaraj Temple and Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves.
  2. Day 2: Drive to Konark for the Sun Temple, then on to Puri for sunset at the beach.
  3. Day 3: Puri Jagannath Temple and Chilika Lake boat ride before drop-off.
North Odisha Coastal Trip 4D/3N · from ₹13,000 pp

Balasore (Chandipur, Talasari, Panchalingeswar) and Bhitarkanika's mangroves.

  1. Day 1: Arrive Balasore, evening at Chandipur's "vanishing sea".
  2. Day 2: Talasari Beach and Panchalingeswar hilltop temple.
  3. Day 3: Boat safari at Bhitarkanika for crocodiles and mangroves.
  4. Day 4: Leisure morning, drop-off.
Tribal & Hill Adventure 5D/4N · from ₹16,500 pp

Daringbadi, Koraput, Deomali and tribal village exploration in the Eastern Ghats.

  1. Day 1: Arrive Daringbadi, the "Kashmir of Odisha" — pine forests and waterfalls.
  2. Day 2: Drive to Koraput, visit the Tribal Museum.
  3. Day 3: Deomali Hills sunrise trek, Odisha's highest peak.
  4. Day 4: Tribal village visit and local craft demonstration.
  5. Day 5: Leisure morning, drop-off.
Every package includes vehicle, driver and fuel. Accommodation and permits (for Similipal / Bhitarkanika) are quoted separately — get in touch for a custom price.
